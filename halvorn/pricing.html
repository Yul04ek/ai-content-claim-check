About Halvorn

Halvorn started in January 2025 as a small script that posted release notes for one product. Today it hosts changelogs and roadmaps for a few dozen small projects.

It is built and run by one person in Lisbon, in the evenings. The product is paid for by its users, there is no outside funding to mention, and the plan is to keep it small and calm.
