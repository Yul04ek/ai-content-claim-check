About this product

Most small products ship updates in silence. People who signed up months ago never find out what changed, and they quietly leave.

Halvorn keeps a public changelog, a simple roadmap and a request board in one place. You write rough notes, press one button, and the writing helper turns them into a readable update. Subscribers get an email automatically.

It is made for one-person products and very small teams. There are no annual contracts, and you can export everything as plain text at any time.

What you get:

  • A public changelog page on your own subdomain
  • A roadmap with three columns: now, next, later
  • A request board where users can vote
  • A writing helper for rough notes (limited on the free plan)
  • Email updates to subscribers

Pricing

Free - $0 - 1 project - public changelog, request board, limited writing helper

Pro - $12 / month - unlimited projects, roadmap, email subscribers, custom domain

Team plans are not available yet.
