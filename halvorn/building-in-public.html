Product Hunt is done. 1 sign-up, 0 MRR. What now?

Posted by the maker on 20 Oct 2026

So the launch day came and went.

I did everything people on here say you should do. Wrote a long post. Pinged friends. Replied to every comment. Set an alarm for 8 in the morning and sat there refreshing the page like it was a lottery.

Result: 9 followers, 1 sign-up, and the one sign-up was my cousin.

Honestly I am not sure what I expected. I have been building Halvorn since January, mostly in the evenings after work, and I think I had a number in my head that was just made up.

For anyone who has not seen it, Halvorn is a changelog and roadmap page for small products. I wrote it because my own users never knew what I had fixed.

A few things I keep going back and forth on:

  • The landing page says changelog, roadmap and requests. Maybe that is three products in a trench coat.
  • Someone at a meetup last week asked if I had a deck. I did not know what to say. I have a Notion page with screenshots.
  • I do not know if talking to investors even makes sense for something this small. Bootstrapping feels more honest but also slower.
  • Pricing: $12 for Pro feels right to me, but nobody has told me either way.

My rule for now: no new features until 5 people pay.

Is that stupid?
