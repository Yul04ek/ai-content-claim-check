Is there an easy way to remove a document when I’m working in the Documents area? In this test, I looked for a delete option but could not find one and I did not delete anything. This suggests the intended product behavior and permissions around deletion are not clear, which could leave users unsure how to remove an unwanted document or whether archiving is the only option.
