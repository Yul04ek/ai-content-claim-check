Opening question: When you try to update your StudioFlow context through the Company DNA workflow, what happens if the final apply step can’t complete?

During testing, a 40% working draft appeared and stayed after refresh; the accepted Company DNA remained at 0%. The approval was saved, but the final apply step failed, and the same recovery action kept failing. The screen showed Approved / Final apply pending, while the accepted version history indicated no completed final apply. Draft fields also contained prompt instructions like “Show me a draft for review” and “Please record only these two product areas,” instead of clean company facts.

This could matter because it blocks updates and could place prompt instructions into company facts if applied as-is. We suggest exploring a reliable recovery path that completes the approved update, with a clear cancel/edit option and separation of draft prompts from company facts.
