Autumn 2026 cohort spotlight: Tallowick

Cohort news, 15 September 2026, by Ilse Varga

Northpier Ventures is a B2B accelerator. Nine companies joined our Autumn 2026 cohort. This post introduces one of them.

About the cohort

The founders in this cohort are at the earliest stage of building a company. Each team works with one partner for twelve weeks on its first customers and its go-to-market plan.

The programme ends with an investor day in December 2026. After it the teams start fundraising with the support of the Northpier team.

Tallowick

Founders
Maren Oduya (CEO) and Tomas Brandl (CTO)
Stage
Closed pilot

Tallowick shows engineering teams where the token spend of their coding agents goes. It reads agent session logs and assigns the spend to tasks, repositories and pull requests, so a team can see which work the bill paid for.

“Maren ran platform teams for eight years and Tomas built the logging pipeline at his last company. They came to us with three pilot teams already using an early script. What they do not have yet is a pricing model or a financial plan, and that is what we will work on first.”

Ilse Varga, Partner, Northpier Ventures
