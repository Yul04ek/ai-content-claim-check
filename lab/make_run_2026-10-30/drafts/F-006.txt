Did you ever try to finalize a small update and hit a stuck moment where the system wouldn’t complete the final step?

During testing, a 40% working draft appeared and stayed after refresh; the approved Company DNA remained at 0%. The final apply step failed, and the same recovery action loop returned the same failure. The screen kept showing Approved / Final apply pending, and the version history indicated no proposal completed the final apply. The draft fields included instruction prompts like “Show me a draft for review” and “Please record only these two product areas,” rather than clean company facts.

This may mean that users cannot finish an approved Company DNA update and could be pulled back into a looping recovery flow, with a risk that prompt instructions get saved as company facts if applied uncorrected. One option is to make the recovery action reliably complete the already-approved update without requiring a new approval, keep the approved payload available, provide a clear cancel/edit route, and prevent prompt instructions from being stored as company facts.
