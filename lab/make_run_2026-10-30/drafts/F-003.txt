Did you ever try to tidy your documents by moving something to archive, only to see it stay where it is? In this test, archiving a document from Documents failed: the request returned 503, so the document stayed visible and did not show up in the archive.

This may matter because users rely on archiving to organize their active list and remove items from view, but the action did not provide a clear result. A possible next step is to fix the archive action in the next release and provide a clear success or failure state so users aren’t left unsure about a document’s status.
